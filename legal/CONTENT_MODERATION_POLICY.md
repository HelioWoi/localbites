## 1. Purpose

This Content Moderation Policy outlines the standards for content uploaded to MenuLove and our procedures for handling inappropriate, illegal, or harmful content.

All Partners and users must comply with this policy. Violations may result in content removal, account suspension, or termination.

---

## 2. Prohibited Content

### 2.1 Illegal Content
Content that violates Australian law, including but not limited to:
- Child exploitation material (mandatory reporting to AFP)
- Terrorism or violent extremism
- Drug trafficking or illegal substances
- Weapons or explosives
- Fraud or scams
- Copyright or trademark infringement
- Defamation

### 2.2 Adult Content - STRICTLY PROHIBITED
**Zero Tolerance Policy:**
- Nudity (partial or full)
- Sexual content or acts
- Pornography or sexually explicit material
- Sexual solicitation
- Sexually suggestive content
- Adult entertainment references

**Immediate Action:** Content removed instantly + account termination

### 2.3 Violence and Gore
- Graphic violence or injury
- Blood or gore
- Animal cruelty
- Self-harm or suicide
- Threats of violence
- Glorification of violence

### 2.4 Hate Speech and Discrimination
- Racial, ethnic, or religious hatred
- Discrimination based on:
  - Gender or sexual orientation
  - Disability
  - Age
  - National origin
- Harassment or bullying
- Derogatory slurs or symbols

### 2.5 Dangerous or Harmful Content
- Instructions for dangerous activities
- Promotion of eating disorders
- Dangerous food challenges
- Misinformation about food safety or allergies
- Fake health claims
- Promotion of illegal substances

### 2.6 Misleading Content
- False advertising or pricing
- Fake reviews or ratings
- Impersonation of others
- Misleading food descriptions
- Hidden allergens or ingredients
- Fake business information

### 2.7 Spam and Manipulation
- Repetitive or spam content
- Fake engagement or views
- Misleading links or redirects
- Malware or phishing attempts
- Automated posting

### 2.8 Inappropriate for Food Platform
- Content unrelated to food or dining
- Political or religious propaganda
- Excessive profanity
- Disturbing or shocking content
- Poor quality or blurry videos

## 3. Content Standards for Partners

### 3.1 Food Content Requirements
All food content must:
- Accurately represent menu items
- Show actual food served at the restaurant
- Include accurate pricing (inc. GST)
- Disclose allergens clearly
- Comply with FSANZ labeling requirements
- Not make false health claims

### 3.2 Video Quality Standards
- Minimum 720p resolution recommended
- Clear audio (if applicable)
- Good lighting
- Stable footage
- Appropriate length (15 seconds - 3 minutes)

### 3.3 Hygiene and Safety
Content must not show:
- Unsanitary food preparation
- Violation of food safety standards
- Expired or spoiled food
- Cross-contamination
- Unsafe food handling

## 4. Reporting Mechanism

### 4.1 How to Report
Users can report inappropriate content via:
- In-app "Report" button
- Email: contact@menulove.com.au
- Subject: "Content Report - [Video ID]"

### 4.2 What to Include
- Link or ID of content
- Reason for report
- Additional details
- Your contact (kept confidential)

### 4.3 Anonymous Reporting
Reports can be submitted anonymously.

---

## 5. Review Process

### 5.1 Response Times

**Critical (Immediate):**
- Illegal content (child exploitation, terrorism)
- Removed instantly + law enforcement notified
- Account suspended

**High Priority (Within 4 hours):**
- Adult content, violence, hate speech
- Removed immediately
- Account warning or suspension

**Medium Priority (Within 24 hours):**
- Misleading content, spam
- Review and possible removal
- Warning issued

**Low Priority (Within 72 hours):**
- Quality issues, minor violations
- Partner notified
- No immediate removal

### 5.2 Appeals
Partners may appeal within 14 days:
- Email contact@localbites.com.au
- Include video ID and explanation
- Review within 7 business days
- Decision is final

---

## 6. Enforcement Actions

### 6.1 First Violation
- Content removed
- Email warning
- Educational resources
- No account penalty

### 6.2 Second Violation
- Content removed
- Written warning
- 7-day upload restriction
- Mandatory policy review

### 6.3 Third Violation
- Content removed
- 30-day suspension
- All content reviewed
- No refund

### 6.4 Severe Violations
- Immediate termination
- No refund
- Permanent ban
- Law enforcement notification (if applicable)
