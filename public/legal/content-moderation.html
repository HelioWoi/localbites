1. Purpose

This Content Moderation Policy outlines the standards for content uploaded to MenuLove and our procedures for handling inappropriate, illegal, or harmful content.

All Partners and users must comply with this policy. Violations may result in content removal, account suspension, or termination.

🚫 ZERO TOLERANCE - Prohibited Content

The following content is STRICTLY PROHIBITED and will result in immediate account termination:

  • Adult Content: Nudity (partial or full), sexual content, pornography, sexually explicit material
  • Child Exploitation: Any content involving minors (mandatory reporting to AFP)
  • Illegal Content: Terrorism, drug trafficking, weapons, fraud
  • Violence: Graphic violence, gore, blood, animal cruelty
  • Hate Speech: Racial, ethnic, religious hatred, discrimination

Action: Content removed instantly + account terminated + law enforcement notified (where applicable)

2. Prohibited Content Categories

2.1 Illegal Content

  • Child exploitation material
  • Terrorism or violent extremism
  • Drug trafficking or illegal substances
  • Weapons or explosives
  • Fraud or scams
  • Copyright or trademark infringement
  • Defamation

2.2 Adult Content - STRICTLY PROHIBITED

  • Nudity (partial or full)
  • Sexual content or acts
  • Pornography or sexually explicit material
  • Sexual solicitation
  • Sexually suggestive content

2.3 Violence and Gore

  • Graphic violence or injury
  • Blood or gore
  • Animal cruelty
  • Self-harm or suicide
  • Threats of violence

2.4 Hate Speech and Discrimination

  • Racial, ethnic, or religious hatred
  • Discrimination based on gender, sexual orientation, disability, age, or national origin
  • Harassment or bullying
  • Derogatory slurs or symbols

2.5 Misleading Content

  • False advertising or pricing
  • Fake reviews or ratings
  • Impersonation of others
  • Misleading food descriptions
  • Hidden allergens or ingredients

3. Content Standards for Partners

3.1 Food Content Requirements

All food content must:

  • Accurately represent menu items
  • Show actual food served at the restaurant
  • Include accurate pricing (inc. GST)
  • Disclose allergens clearly
  • Comply with FSANZ labeling requirements
  • Not make false health claims

3.2 Video Quality Standards

  • Minimum 720p resolution recommended
  • Clear audio (if applicable)
  • Good lighting
  • Stable footage
  • Appropriate length (15 seconds - 3 minutes)

4. Reporting Mechanism

4.1 How to Report

Users can report inappropriate content via:

  • In-app "Report" button on any video
  • Email: contact@menulove.com.au
  • Subject: "Content Report - [Video ID]"

4.2 What to Include

  • Link or ID of content
  • Reason for report (select from categories)
  • Additional details (optional)
  • Your contact information (kept confidential)

Anonymous Reporting: Reports can be submitted anonymously.

5. Review Process

5.1 Response Times

Critical (Immediate):

  • Illegal content (child exploitation, terrorism)
  • Removed instantly + law enforcement notified
  • Account suspended pending investigation

High Priority (Within 4 hours):

  • Adult content, violence, hate speech
  • Content removed immediately
  • Account warning or suspension

Medium Priority (Within 24 hours):

  • Misleading content, spam
  • Content review and possible removal
  • Warning issued to Partner

Low Priority (Within 72 hours):

  • Quality issues, minor violations
  • Partner notified to update content
  • No immediate removal

6. Enforcement Actions

6.1 First Violation

  • Content removed
  • Email warning sent
  • Educational resources provided
  • No account penalty

6.2 Second Violation

  • Content removed
  • Written warning
  • 7-day content upload restriction
  • Mandatory policy review

6.3 Third Violation

  • Content removed
  • 30-day account suspension
  • All content reviewed
  • Subscription continues (no refund)

6.4 Severe or Repeated Violations

  • Immediate account termination
  • No refund of subscription fees
  • Permanent ban from platform
  • Law enforcement notification (if applicable)

⚠️ Appeals Process

Partners may appeal content removal within 14 days:

  1. Email contact@menulove.com.au
  2. Include video ID and explanation
  3. We review within 7 business days
  4. Decision is final

7. Legal Obligations

7.1 Mandatory Reporting

We are required by Australian law to report:

  • Child exploitation material
  • Terrorism content
  • Serious criminal activity

7.2 Cooperation with Authorities

We will cooperate with:

  • Australian Federal Police (AFP)
  • eSafety Commissioner
  • State police
  • Regulatory authorities

8. Partner Responsibilities

Partners are solely responsible for:

  • All content they upload
  • Compliance with this policy
  • Legal consequences of violations
  • Copyright and licensing
  • Accuracy of information

MenuLove is not responsible for Partner content.
